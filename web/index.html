Election Lens

How America voted, 2000 to 2024

Every presidential election since 2000, from the Electoral College down to each county. Pick a year, click a state to see its counties, then click a county to see how it has voted over time.

    Sources: 2000–2016 official county returns from the MIT Election Data and Science Lab; 2020–2024 county results compiled by Tony McGovern. Alaska is shown statewide. Connecticut reports 2024 results by planning region.
