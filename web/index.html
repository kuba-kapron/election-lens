Election Lens

How America voted, 2000 to 2024

Every presidential election since 2000, from the Electoral College down to each county. Pick a year, click a state to see its counties, then click a county to see how it has voted over time.

    President: 2000–2016 official county returns from the MIT Election Data and Science Lab; 2020–2024 county results compiled by Tony McGovern. Alaska is shown statewide. Connecticut reports 2024 presidential results by planning region.

    Senate and House: certified results from the Federal Election Commission (2020, 2022) and the Clerk of the House (2024), checked against each other; Senate county figures from the MIT Election Data and Science Lab precinct returns; district maps from the US Census Bureau and the North Carolina General Assembly.

    Party icons from Wikimedia Commons: Democratic and Republican public domain; Libertarian by Hdebug (original by Lance W. Haverkamp) and Green by Gappalachia, both CC BY-SA 4.0, resized.
