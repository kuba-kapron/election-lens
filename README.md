# Election Lens

Election Lens is an open-source website for exploring election results at every level, from the whole country down to counties and municipalities, and for following new elections live.

## Status

Early development. First milestone: live tracking of key races in the US midterm elections on 3 November 2026.

## Scope

**Now**
- United States: presidential results by county, 2000–2024
- Live results on election night for:
  - Senate: Texas, Michigan, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Florida, Louisiana, Mississippi, Maine, Ohio
  - Governor: Ohio, Texas

**Next**
- Certified 2026 US results and House/Senate history
- Poland: historical results down to gmina level, and live tracking for the 2027 parliamentary election

**Later**
- More countries, party pages, comparisons and swing maps, left–right trends over time

## Data sources

- [MIT Election Data and Science Lab](https://electionlab.mit.edu/data): US election returns
- [OpenElections](https://github.com/openelections): US official results
- US Census TIGER/Line: county and district boundaries
- State election offices: live election-night results
- Państwowa Komisja Wyborcza (PKW): Polish official results

Each dataset keeps its original license and terms; see the source for details.
